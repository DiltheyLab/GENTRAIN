We are pleased to announce the release of the new **GENTRAIN User Manual**! 📘

Download the manual here: [UserManual.pdf](/blog/Nutzungshandbuch.pdf)

The manual was developed by the **University of Bielefeld**, one of our **project partners in GENTRAIN**, and provides a **comprehensive description of the application** as well as a **practical introduction** to using the software effectively.

<!-- truncate -->

The user manual is designed for **end users** who wish to use GENTRAIN in their daily work — for example, in **public health departments**, **research institutions**, or **infection surveillance**.  
Its goal is to make it easier to get started with the software and to explain the most important features in a clear and structured way.

Included in the manual:

- A **step-by-step introduction** to the GENTRAIN interface
- Detailed instructions on **data import, visualization, and outbreak analysis**
- Information on **data protection, session handling, and storage options**
- Explanations of **key concepts and terms** used throughout the application

The user manual is now available through the documentation platform and complements the existing sections of the online documentation.

We would like to thank the **University of Bielefeld** for their dedicated collaboration and valuable contribution to improving the usability of GENTRAIN.
