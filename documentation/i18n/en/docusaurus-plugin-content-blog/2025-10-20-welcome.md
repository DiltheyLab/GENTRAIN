Welcome to the official **GENTRAIN documentation**! 👋

**GENTRAIN** is an innovative software solution for **genetically based tracing of infection chains**.  
![GENTRAIN](/blog/gentrain_promo.png)
It was developed at the **Universtitätsklinikum Düsseldorf** as part of a research project commissioned by the **Ministry of Labour, Health and Social Affairs of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia** and funded by the **European Union (NextGenerationEU)**.

The goal of GENTRAIN is to support public health authorities and research institutions in **understanding infection chains**, analyzing outbreaks more efficiently, and visualizing infection dynamics.

<!-- truncate -->

The application consists of three main components:

- 🧬 **Dashboard** – Visualizes genetic distances between cases as a Minimum Spanning Tree (MST).
- 🧠 **Outbreak Analysis** – Provides tools for targeted outbreak analysis and automated report generation.
- 📂 **Data Management** – Enables importing, managing, and securely deleting case, sequence, and contact data.

The documentation is divided into three main sections:

- **Application:** How to use GENTRAIN as an end user.
- **Development:** Technical documentation covering architecture, data handling, APIs, and development guidelines.
- **Administration:** Guidance on deployment, configuration, user management, and operational maintenance.

We invite you to explore GENTRAIN – a modern, research-driven solution that advances digital infection control.  
We hope you enjoy working with GENTRAIN!
